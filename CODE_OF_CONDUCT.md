# Code of conduct

This is a small technical project. A few rules keep it pleasant to work on.

- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith, and say plainly when you disagree.
- No harassment, insults, or sexual or discriminatory language or imagery, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else the project is discussed.
- Do not post anyone's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block accounts that break these rules.

To report a problem, email support@gpulogy.com. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept private.
